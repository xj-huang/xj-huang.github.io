Welcome!

This is Xinjie Huang. I am a PhD candidate in Civil and Environmental Engineering at Princeton University. I am also a Science, Technology, and Environmental Policy (STEP) Fellow at the High Meadows Environmental Institute (HMEI) at Princeton University, and a board member on Board on Urban Environment at American Meteorological Society.

As an urban climatologist with interdisciplinary training in engineering and policy, I place humans at the center of urban environmental modeling, treating health risk as the endpoint of urban physical processes and evaluating technologies through integrated heat-pollution assessment. This framework bridges global land-use and emission datasets, mesoscale climate-chemistry models (WRF/WRF-Chem), urban land surface schemes (urban canopy models, UCMs), and human thermo-physiological models.
